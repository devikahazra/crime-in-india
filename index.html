Indicator definitions

Source: NCRB Geography: 2011 Census districts Years 2014 → 2019 1,013 district-name variants resolved

Method. District-level NCRB records aggregated to the 2011 Census district. Districts joined by name within state, using a crosswalk built from 2001-2019 NCRB releases; post-2011 splits (e.g. Hapur out of Ghaziabad, Mewat/Nuh out of Gurgaon) are folded into their 2011 parent. Telangana districts keep their own pc11 identity for display, though pc11 itself predates Telangana and codes them under undivided Andhra Pradesh.

Years shown. 2014-2019. 2016 uses a coarser NCRB reporting schema than the other years (35 IPC categories vs. 118+); finer-grained years are summed to match wherever the underlying columns allow it. Cyber Crimes, Juvenile offences, and Missing Persons are only available from 2017 onward — NCRB did not publish district-level figures for these before then.

Repaired entries. Several source-file spelling variants (e.g. "Andrha Pradesh," "Harayana") were matched back to their correct state; railway and city-police jurisdictions with no single corresponding district (e.g. Cyberabad, statewide "Railways" entries) were dropped rather than guessed at.

See "Indicator definitions" above for how Total, Per capita, and Avg per 100k are computed.
